Arizona Food-Security Funders
Starter list — customize as you learn more.

Arizona Community Foundation (ACF)

Statewide · Community grants, endowments, donor-advised funds

Multiple grant programs including community grants for food security. Deadlines rolling and cycle-based. Strong fit for capacity building.

azfoundation.org →

CFSA CORE Support (Community Foundation for Southern AZ)

Southern AZ · General operating support

Core operating grants — one of the few sources for unrestricted funding. Small nonprofits welcome.

cfsaz.org →

Vitalyst Health Foundation

Statewide · Health equity, food access

Food-as-medicine and community food access are priorities. Alignment with SDoH framing helps.

vitalysthealth.org →

Piper Trust (Virginia G. Piper Charitable Trust)

Maricopa County · Basic needs, capacity

Larger foundation with a basic-needs focus. Competitive but attainable with strong narrative.

pipertrust.org →

Feeding America — Local Food Bank Grants (via St. Mary's)

Through your partner network

Ask St. Mary's Food Bank Alliance about pass-through Feeding America grants for member agencies. Often available for equipment.

Walmart Community Grants — Local

Local Walmart & Sam's Club stores · $250–$5,000

Apply through each local store manager. Quick turnaround, good for one-time needs (freezer, shelving, distribution supplies).

walmart.org →

Kroger / Fry's Zero Hunger Zero Waste

Local + national · Food rescue focus

Fry's is Kroger in AZ. Food rescue partnerships + community grants. Reach out to your local store's community engagement contact.

City of Maricopa — Community Grants

Municipal · Local programs

Check the city's annual community grant cycle. As a local nonprofit serving Maricopa residents, alignment is strong.

Pinal County — CDBG / community funds

County · Federal pass-through + local funds

Community Development Block Grant pass-throughs for food security infrastructure. Ask county community services department.
