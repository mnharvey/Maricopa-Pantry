📅 First, create the Google Calendar

Takes about 5 minutes. Only needs to be done once.

  1. Go to calendar.google.com signed in as the Pantry account (ideally the one that will own Google for Nonprofits).
  2. In the left sidebar, click the + next to "Other calendars" → Create new calendar.
  3. Name it Pantry Grants & IT. Add a description if you want.
  4. After it's created, click it in the sidebar → Settings and sharing.
  5. Under Access permissions for events, decide: tick "Make available to public" if clients should see it too (not usually needed for grants/IT), or leave unticked and share with specific team members by email.
  6. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy the Calendar ID (looks like a long email address ending in @group.calendar.google.com).
  7. Paste it below.
Paste from Google Calendar → Settings → Integrate calendar → Calendar ID

Subscribe once — stay in sync forever

Tap the button for the calendar app you use. Every current and future event syncs to your phone automatically.
